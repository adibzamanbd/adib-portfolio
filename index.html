I’m Adib Zaman, an independent creative developer and digital artist who likes making creative design.

My work moves between frontend development, interactive experiences, visual design, image editing, and identity creation. I use technology as material: sometimes precise, sometimes strange, always in service of an idea.

I’m drawn to expressive motion, unusual compositions, rich color relationships, and digital experiences that feel considered instead of assembled from a template.

I use AI as a creative assistant—not a replacement for my own judgment. I still want to understand the code, question the result, and take responsibility for what I put into the world.
